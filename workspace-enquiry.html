Studio workspace enquiry

Copa + Glas is an East London design studio working in copper and hand-cut glass. We make mirrors and lighting by hand, to order, for interior designers, architects, and private collectors.

We are currently looking for a workshop space in East London, between 250 and 450 square feet, suitable for light craft and making work. Ideally the space would offer good natural light, sufficient ceiling height for working with larger pieces, and an environment we can use as both a working studio and an occasional client meeting space. We would also be interested in a space that could potentially be used for a day to shoot a short film — the same industrial workshop style as shown in the enclosed images.

Copa + Glas is an established, trading studio with confirmed commissions and a growing client base that includes interior design practices and private clients across London and internationally.

We are looking to move into a suitable space as soon as the right opportunity arises, on a flexible or fixed term licence, and are open to discussion on terms.

Please find enclosed images showing the type of environment we are looking for. If you have anything suitable or know of any upcoming availability, we would be very glad to hear from you.
